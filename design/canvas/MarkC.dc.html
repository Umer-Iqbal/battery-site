Hex Cell

One unbroken stroke, drawn as a hexagon with the top vertex opened and the two ends deliberately offset. A cell seen from above, a solar module, a wheel hub — and the green end is the live terminal.

Why this one

Built for hardware. A single constant-weight stroke survives being etched into a battery case, screen-printed on a bike downtube, or embroidered — and it is already square, so it is an app icon with no extra work.

The tradeoff

Hexagons are everywhere in this sector. The offset opening is what makes it ownable — and that is exactly the detail that closes up below about 20px. Look hard at the 16px sample.
