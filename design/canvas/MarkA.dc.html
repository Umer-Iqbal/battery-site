Option A

Charge E

An E whose three arms are cut on the same diagonal the old NexVolt N used. Read one way it is a letter; read another it is a charge gauge, or three stacked battery modules.

Why this one

The most legible of the three and the closest to what your customers already recognise. Holds together at 16px with no detail loss.

The tradeoff

Letter marks are crowded in energy — this is competent rather than distinctive, and it says nothing about bikes or solar.
